01Green flag / Live Mode

Live Mode is your race control room.

See the drivers who matter around you, how your pace is evolving, what the tyres and fuel are doing, and when the next stop becomes unavoidable.

You decide

Push, defend, manage traffic, hold target pace, save fuel, protect tyres, or prepare the pit stop.

A

Race position & traffic

See the drivers that can affect your race now.

+
  • Up to six drivers ahead and six behind
  • Current driver always visible and highlighted
  • Position, laps, pace context, gaps, and pit state
  • Dynamic window follows gains and losses
  • Full standings remain available separately
You decide

Push, defend, manage traffic, or stop spending time on a car that is no longer relevant.

B

Track, pace & lap context

Understand whether the race is coming to you or moving away.

+
  • Circle map and car positions
  • Current, previous, best, and rolling pace
  • Sector performance and lap validity
  • Session history, X-Y plotting, and lap analysis
  • Traffic clusters and live session state
You decide

Push, settle into target pace, or change the approach to protect the stint.

C

Tyres, fuel & stint health

Know the condition of the car before the car tells you.

+
  • Fuel remaining, per-lap use, range, and reserve
  • Tyre temperature, wear, and projected wear at stop
  • Brake temperature where available
  • Stint duration and trend-based warnings
  • Measured average and confidence
You decide

Continue, save, shorten the stint, or bring the stop forward.

Live tools

All the context needed without changing tools.

+
  • Live dashboard and circle map
  • Lap stats and nearby-driver standings
  • Session history and X-Y plotting
  • Driver Coach and pit window
  • Session settings and source context
In one sentence

Live Mode tells you what is happening around the car, what will happen to the stint, and what decision matters next.

02Before & during / Plan Mode

Build the stint before it builds you.

Plan Mode turns race duration, pace, fuel use, tyre wear, pit loss, service time, and reserves into strategies you can actually execute.

A

Model status

See every assumption and its confidence.

+
  • Pace model and trend
  • Fuel use, variance, and reserve
  • Tyre wear and start state
  • Calculated start fuel
  • Service model and source labels
You decide

Whether the plan is ready to use or needs a safer assumption.

B

Live adaptation

Recalculate from what the race is actually doing.

+
  • Update pace from recent clean laps
  • Update consumption and tyre trends
  • Recalculate when a stop changes
  • Compare alternative pit laps
  • Explain the change in recommendation
You decide

Pit now, extend, save fuel, change service, or select an alternative plan.

C

Strategy comparison

Compare timing, risk, stops, and execution.

+
  • Conservative, attack, saving, and preservation options
  • Projected total time and stop count
  • Tyre phases and fuel service
  • Risk and execution difficulty
  • Selected-plan state
You decide

Which strategy fits the race you are actually running.

Do not just see the plan. Understand why it changed.

03Chequered flag / Driver Mode

Find the time loss. Fix one thing.

Driver Mode identifies repeatable patterns, shows the evidence, and gives one clear action for the next run—without dumping every graph on the driver.

01 / Detect

Find repeatable time loss.

02 / Compare

Use the strongest clean pattern.

03 / Act

Leave with one specific adjustment.

A

Repeatable loss detection

Separate a recurring weakness from a messy lap.

+
  • Patterns across multiple valid laps
  • Comparison to your own clean baseline
  • Opportunity ranked by time and confidence
  • Entry, apex, exit, brake, throttle, steering, and gear context
You avoid

Practising a conclusion built from one bad sample.

What happened: the loss is named and located.
Why it matters: the effect through the corner is explained.
Try this next: one concise instruction.
Show evidence: synchronized traces and segment context.

04Deep review / Analysis Mode

The whole session. Still connected.

Open a local or imported session and inspect every relevant channel without losing the race context that gives the data meaning.

Bad telemetry should not become a bad conclusion.

Quality controls protect coaching and personal records from corrupted context. Impossible values are rejected or flagged, spikes and outliers are identified, expected ranges are checked against the car, circuit, session, and lap, and uncertain findings stay visibly uncertain.

  • Reject impossible values
  • Flag sudden spikes and outliers
  • Protect maxima from isolated errors
  • Validate session and lap context
  • Mark low-confidence findings
  • Exclude invalid laps from records

Every validated session becomes part of a searchable archive, linked back to the exact lap and evidence that created each result.

Your best lap is not a number. It is a lap you can reopen.
