01Green flag / Live mode

Know the race you’re in.

Live Mode keeps position, pace, traffic, inputs, tyre condition, fuel range, and the next pit decision in one calm race-control view. The signal stays useful when the stint gets noisy.

Driver context

Circle map, lap stats, standings, session history, X-Y plotting, and lap analysis make the field and your own pace readable from every angle.

Race control

Watch the pit window evolve, keep the settings tuned to your session, and move from raw timing to the next decision without changing tools.

02Before and during / Plan mode

Build the stint before it builds you.

Model race duration, pace, fuel use, tyre wear, service time, reserves, and pit-lane loss from live data or a recorded session. Then compare executable plans, not hopeful guesses.

Race assumptions stay visible and editable. Pace, fuel, tyre, reserve, and service models show their source and confidence so you can understand why the plan changed—not just that it changed.

03Chequered flag / Corner coach

Turn traces into one clear action.

Session Review finds repeatable time loss, compares it against your strongest clean pattern, and turns the evidence into a specific instruction for the next run.

01 / Detect

04 / Deep review

The whole session. Still connected.

Open a recorded session from the local database and inspect its complete channel set: lap validity, fuel, speed, RPM, inputs, assists, G-force, brakes, tyres, GPS, and metadata.

Offline CSV analysis brings MoTeC-style exploration into the same workflow, while session verdicts and prioritised gains keep the review focused on what matters next.

05Across every drive / Profile

A career you can query.

Best laps link back to their exact session and source lap. Search and filter your history by track, layout, car, class, fuel, tyre wear, temperature, and speed—then reopen the evidence behind the number.

Your own racing archive.

Career overview, most-used cars, most-driven tracks, distance by class, and every validated personal best turn scattered sessions into a driver history with memory.
