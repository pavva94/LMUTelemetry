01During the session
Real-time operating environment

Read the race. Not every number.

When you are driving, you need the right information at the right moment. Live Mode filters and organises the active session so threats, trends, car state, and the next decision stay clear.

3.1 / Primary race screen

Live Dashboard

One compact operating view connects the field around you with your car’s current ability to finish the stint.

NearbyDrivers ahead and behind, current driver highlighted, gains and losses visible as the window moves.
PaceThree-lap and seven-lap pace distinguish one quick lap from a meaningful trend.
Race contextPosition, gaps, lap state, flags, inputs, and relevant session status.
Car stateTyre temperatures and balance, fuel to finish, surplus or deficit, and pit guidance.
The decision

Push, defend, manage, save, extend, or stop — without scanning an engineering wall while driving.

Complete live workflow

Seven pages. One active session.

The dashboard is the centre, but Live Mode also lets you move from immediate context to full-field, spatial, historical, channel-level, strategy, and coaching views without leaving the live workflow.

  • Comparable runs remain separate and readable

  • Select X and Y telemetry fields

  • Keep units and source context visible

Move from a fixed dashboard to a question-driven graph without exporting the active session.

06

Live Pit-Stop Window

Turn consumption and wear into a practical stop range.

  • Fuel remaining and measured use per lap
  • Fuel required to finish, surplus, or deficit
  • Estimated fuel- and tyre-limited pit laps
  • Projected tyre condition at the stop
  • Whether the current plan can reach the finish
The decision

Pit now, extend, save fuel, change service, or stay out because the current plan still closes.

07

Driver Coach

Translate repeatable lap evidence into one next action.

  • Find repeatable losses across valid laps
  • Rank opportunities by time and confidence
  • Separate entry, apex, and exit behaviour
  • Compare speed, brake, throttle, steering, and gear traces
  • Present one concise instruction with evidence
Detect · compare · act

Practise the highest-value repeatable issue, not a conclusion built from one messy lap.

02Before and after
Strategy & session analysis

Prepare the next race. Explain the last one.

Plan Mode uses real telemetry, not anonymous defaults. Load the active session, a completed session, saved telemetry, or a matching historical stint, then make every assumption visible.

5.1 / Future race

Strategy Planner

Build a race whose duration and conditions may differ from the original data, while preserving the evidence source behind pace, fuel, tyre, and service assumptions.

ConfigureDuration, starting conditions, capacity, starting fuel, compounds, degradation, wear limits, pit loss, service time, pace, variation, stint length, and constraints.
SourceActive session, completed session, saved telemetry, historical stints, or selected car-and-track evidence.
CompareStop count, timing, fuel progression, tyre phases, total time, risk, and execution difficulty.
Two planning lenses

Fast logic and uncertainty.

Use the heuristic planner when you need clear, quick alternatives. Use Monte Carlo simulation when variation matters and a distribution is more honest than one predicted finish.

01 / Heuristic

Understandable strategy structures

Quickly evaluate logically constructed combinations and expose why a strategy is viable.

  • Fast calculations and clear alternatives
  • Pit timing and executable stint structure
  • Readable recommended plan
  • Useful for rapid iteration
02 / Monte Carlo

A range of possible races

Model many outcomes across variation in pace, consumption, degradation, stops, traffic, consistency, and stint execution.

  • Best-case, expected, and worst-case ranges
  • Total-time and strategy distributions
  • Fuel and tyre confidence intervals
  • Probability-aware comparison
Plans that move with the race

Recalculate from what actually happened.

Recent clean laps can update weighted pace, consumption, wear, traffic risk, and confidence while alternatives remain visible.

The decision

Pit now, extend, save, change service, or switch plan with the reason for the change still in view.

5.2 / Completed session

Session Report

An aggregated review of the active session, a completed session, or selected historical telemetry. The emphasis changes with the job the driver was trying to do.

Practice

Pace development, consistency, stint and setup comparison, fuel-load effects, tyre degradation, long-run pace, simulations, and interrupted laps.

Qualifying

Best and theoretical laps, sector execution, preparation, tyre readiness, traffic impact, time loss, and push-lap consistency.

Race

Result, position change, stint pace, fuel and tyre management, stops, strategy execution, traffic, progression, and important events.

From channels to conclusions.

Summary metrics, lap and stint analysis, pace, fuel and tyre trends, consistency, anomalies, driver suggestions, strategy observations, and session events remain connected to the source telemetry.

03Across every drive
Personal archive & review

Every lap becomes part of your racing history.

Career Mode builds a complete profile from saved LMU telemetry. It is a racing identity grounded in recorded sessions, not manually entered achievements.
